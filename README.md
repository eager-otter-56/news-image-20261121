A one-image summary of the world's news of **Saturday 21 November 2026**, drawn as a newspaper front page:

- **Russia rains fire on Kyiv** — more than 400 drones and 110 missiles overnight; at least eight killed in Kyiv, bridges and power lines smashed, and two missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland.
- **AI rout hits Wall St** — US stocks fell 3.7% as an AI and chip sell-off pushed the S&P 500 and Dow into correction.
- **Trump defends summit** — a 90-minute White House press conference hailing the first Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen and its peace declaration on nuclear risks.
- **COP31 into overtime** — the UN climate summit in Antalya missed its deadline, split over fossil fuels.
- **Cancer pill breakthrough** — the trial pill daraxonrasib made pancreatic cancer undetectable in one in 12 patients.

Entry for the make.fun competition [news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121).

## Run

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to the output directory — `/output` by default. No network, deterministic output.
